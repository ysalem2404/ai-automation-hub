GPO Membership Note

Group Purchasing Organizations negotiate volume discounts on behalf of member hospitals. Suppliers must apply and be approved to join GPO contract portfolios. Major GPOs include Vizient (~$100B purchasing volume), Premier (~$70B), and HealthTrust (~$50B).
