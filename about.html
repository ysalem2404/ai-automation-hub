ABOUT THALASSA US

One accountable lead.

Thalassa US is a founder-led cybersecurity and practical AI advisory firm for growing organizations. No bench, no theater — the person you hire is the person who shows up.

02WHY
FOUNDER-LED

Most consulting firms sell you a senior partner and deliver a junior team. The model here is the opposite: one senior advisor, close to the work from the first session to the final handoff.

That only works with scope discipline — taking on work one focused advisor can credibly deliver, and saying no to the rest. You will never find a 24/7 SOC, a rotating bench, or a client roster that doesn’t exist on this site. What you find is what you get.

When an engagement needs more hands than one advisor — a wider assessment, parallel workstreams, specialized tooling — I bring vetted delivery partners and stay accountable for the outcome. You approve the team. You always know who’s doing the work, and why.
